# Topology

- [Gluing two balls into a sphere](gluing-balls-into-spheres.md): two disks glued along their rims give $S^2$, two balls give $S^3$; paths cross the seam, and a loop through both centres contracts.
- [Antipodal quotients and real projective space](real-projective-space.md): identifying every point of a sphere with its opposite gives $\mathbb{RP}^n$, also drawn as one ball with opposite boundary points identified.
- [SU(2) as the double cover of SO(3), topologically](su2-double-cover-of-so3.md): SU(2) is $S^3$, SO(3) is $\mathbb{RP}^3$, and identifying $U$ with $-U$ maps one onto the other; why 2π is not a closed loop in SU(2) but 4π is.
