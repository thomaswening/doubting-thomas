# Quantum field theory

## Spinor-helicity course

A path from the Dirac equation to QED amplitudes and cross-sections in the spinor-helicity formalism. Read the notes in this order. Conventions follow Schwartz (mostly-minus metric), with Srednicki as the parallel source.

1. [SL(2,ℂ) as the double cover of the Lorentz group](sl2c-double-cover-of-the-lorentz-group.md): four-vectors as Hermitian 2×2 matrices, $`X \mapsto AXA^\dagger`$ as a Lorentz transformation, and why $A$ and $-A$ give the same one.
2. [Left- and right-handed Weyl spinors](weyl-spinor-representations.md): the two inequivalent actions of SL(2,ℂ) on $`\mathbb{C}^2`$, the invariant product $`\psi^T\varepsilon\phi`$, parity, and a light-like momentum as $`\lambda\lambda^\dagger`$.
